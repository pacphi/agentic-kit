## Superpowers with Ruflo/AQE

Superpowers structures the workflow: brainstorming, plans, TDD, debugging, worktrees,
review, and verification. Ruflo supplies persistent memory, routing, measured analysis,
security, and topology-aware coordination. AQE supplies quality-engineering capability
when enabled.

In overlap, use the Superpowers process and the smallest relevant Ruflo/AQE capability:
recall prior decisions before planning, gather evidence before debugging conclusions,
give parallel writers disjoint ownership, and keep verification as the final gate.
Invoking a workflow skill does not prove a tool or service is configured or healthy;
discover and verify it when needed.
