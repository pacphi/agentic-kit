## How these lists were built

**Part 1** is weighted toward people who actually direct engineering and research — CTOs, chief scientists, chief architects, and founder-engineers — rather than pure business executives (CFOs, CROs, etc. are excluded). Entries are grouped by organization rather than strictly ranked.

**Part 2** is for hands-on builders: people who ship code, tools, protocols, libraries, or models. People already on Part 1 (e.g. Karpathy, Chintala, Zoph, Truell) are not repeated.

Both lists are editorial judgments assembled from web research as of the snapshot date. Cross-references include the TIME100 AI 2026 list (published August 27, 2026), Fast Company's AI 20 for 2026, and the AI Accelerator Institute's "25 AI engineers you should be following in 2026" (July 2026).

## Caveats

- **Recency.** Several moves (Zoph to DeepMind, the Cursor close, Pragmatik Labs, Discovery Loop) are days or weeks old; titles and reporting lines may still be settling.
- **Meta TBD Lab entries (Part 2, #45–47)** come from 2025 hiring reports. MSL has since restructured, so treat these as "hired for" rather than "currently leading."
- **Independent practitioner titles** are descriptive rather than official.
- **Editorial selection.** These are not official rankings. Grouping by organization is used instead of a strict 1–50 order.
